opendesk shows a picture next to every agent, contact and company. When there is none, it shows initials. Pictures come from an upload or from an automatic source, and **an upload always wins**.

| Who | Sources, highest first |
| --- | --- |
| Agents | Your upload, then the picture from Google or GitHub when you sign in with them |
| Contacts | An agent's upload, then the picture your site passes to a verified chat widget session, then Gravatar (when turned on) |
| Companies | An agent's upload, then the company website's icon (when turned on) or the logo provider your instance configures |
| Workspace and brands | The logos an admin uploads |

Microsoft and single sign-on accounts carry no picture, so those agents upload one.

## Upload your photo

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your profile">

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Profile**, or go to **Settings, Profile**.

</Step>
<Step title="Change the photo">

Next to **Photo**, click **Change photo** and pick an image. Crop it to the square you want, and it saves. **Remove** brings back the picture from your sign-in, or your initials.

<Screenshot src="/guides/pictures/profile" alt="Settings, Profile: the Photo row with Change photo and Remove ringed as 1" />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Contacts and companies

Any agent can change the picture of a contact or company. Open one from **Contacts**, then click its picture.

<Screenshot src="/guides/pictures/contact" alt="A contact's drawer with the picture ringed as 1 and its menu, Change photo, Refresh and Remove, ringed as 2" />

<Legend>

1. **The picture**: click it for the menu, or drop an image file on it.
2. **Change photo** uploads one. **Refresh** looks for an automatic picture again. **Remove** deletes the upload, and the automatic picture comes back if there is one.

</Legend>

The menu also says where an automatic picture came from, such as **From Gravatar** or **From the company's website**.

<Screenshot src="/guides/pictures/company" alt="A company's drawer with its logo ringed as 1, and the note From the company's website ringed as 2" />

## Workspace logos and automatic pictures

Admins set the workspace's pictures in **Settings, General**.

<Screenshot src="/guides/pictures/workspace" alt="The Logo row in the workspace settings ringed as 1" />

The workspace **Logo** shows in the workspace switcher, in emails and on the help center. The help center header uses the wide **Help center logo** if set, else the workspace logo and name, and the **Favicon** is the icon in the browser tab. Each [brand](/guides/brands-and-widgets/) can set its own in its **Help center** tab.

<Screenshot src="/guides/pictures/help-center" alt="The Help center logo row ringed as 1 and the Favicon row ringed as 2" />

Under **Contact and company pictures**, choose which automatic sources to use.

<Screenshot src="/guides/pictures/sources" alt="Contact and company pictures: Gravatar pictures ringed as 1, off, and Company logos ringed as 2, on" />

<Legend>

1. **Gravatar pictures** is off by default. When on, opendesk sends a hash of each contact's email to Gravatar and uses the picture if one exists. Turning it off removes Gravatar pictures.
2. **Company logos** is on by default. opendesk reads the company domain's home page for its app icon or favicon, and uses the first usable square image of at least 32 px.

</Legend>

<Callout>

Images are PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, at most 5 MB. When your site [verifies a chat visitor](/guides/live-chat/identity-verification/), it can pass `avatar_url` with the session, and opendesk copies that picture to the contact. Unverified sessions never set a picture.

</Callout>

## How pictures are handled

- Every picture is downloaded by the server, never loaded from the original site by your browser or your customers'.
- Only PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP content is accepted, checked from the bytes themselves. SVG and anything else is refused.
- Size is capped before decoding (5 MB, 4096 px a side). Pictures are cropped to a square, resized to 256 and 64 px and saved as PNG without any metadata, so location and camera data never survive.
- Downloads use https only, refuse private and internal network addresses on every redirect, and time out after 10 seconds.
- Pictures are served with long-lived caching and strict headers that stop them from running as anything other than an image.
- Uploads and removals are recorded in the audit log.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Refreshing

Automatic pictures are checked again every 30 days. When a lookup finds nothing, opendesk waits 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 days before trying again, then every 30 days. **Refresh** on a contact or company looks now, as do `POST /v1/contacts/{id}/avatar/refresh` and `POST /v1/companies/{id}/avatar/refresh`.

</Fold>

## Self-hosting

Company logos from websites work with no setup. To use [logo.dev](https://logo.dev) instead, set `LOGO_DEV_TOKEN` (see the [configuration reference](/self-hosting/configuration/#profile-pictures)).
